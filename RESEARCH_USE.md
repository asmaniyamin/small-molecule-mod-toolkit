# Molecular-modeling research use and limitations

Small Molecule Modification Toolkit provides computational molecular graph
construction, editing, fragment merging, and functional-group analysis.
Its outputs are computational representations and must be evaluated in the
context of the intended research application. The project-wide license also
covers protein-related modules when included in a distribution.

Users are responsible for checking input structures, atom and bond properties,
transformations, parameter choices, and outputs, and for arranging appropriate
independent validation before relying on results in synthesis, experiments,
drug discovery, development, or manufacturing. A successful computation does
not demonstrate synthetic feasibility, compound safety, toxicity, biological
activity, protein function or stability, binding affinity, efficacy, or
manufacturability.

For protein-related modules when provided, validation should address the
assumptions relevant to the workflow, such as atom and residue identities,
biological assembly selection, protonation states, missing atoms or residues,
cofactors, and binding-site definitions. These are examples of downstream
validation considerations, not a claim that those capabilities are currently
implemented in this project.
